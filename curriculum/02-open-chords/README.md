# Stage 2 — Open Chords

Goal: hold down and switch between the handful of chords that unlock hundreds of songs.

## Chord order (deliberate — minimizes finger relearning between each)
1. **Em** (easiest chord that exists — 2 fingers)
2. **Am** (one finger moves from Em)
3. **C** (introduces 3-finger shape)
4. **G** (bigger stretch, most awkward at first — don't rush it)
5. **D**
6. **Em7 / Am7** (easier variants, good confidence boosters)
7. **A**
8. **E**

Deliberately *not* doing barre chords (F, B, etc.) yet — those need hand strength you don't
have yet and will just cause frustration. They come after Stage 3.

## Drill pattern per chord
1. Place the chord shape with no strumming — check every string rings clean (app: chord-recognition
   listens via your mic/audio interface and tells you which strings are buzzing/muted)
2. Strum slowly, 1 strum every 2 seconds, reposition fingers between each
3. Once clean: metronome at 60bpm, 1 strum per beat
4. Two-chord switching drills (see below) — this is the actual hard part, not holding the shape

## Switching drills (do these before "songs" — switching is the real skill)
- Em ↔ Am (1 finger moves)
- Am ↔ C
- C ↔ G
- G ↔ D
- Loop each pair for 1-2 minutes at a slow metronome tempo before increasing speed

## Goals to move on
- [ ] All 5 core chords (Em, Am, C, G, D) ring clean individually
- [ ] Can switch between any two adjacent-in-list chords in under 1 second at 70bpm
- [ ] Chord Drill in the app passes comfortably at the "Novice" difficulty tier
  (randomized prompts across Em, Am, C, G, D, Em7, Am7)

## Use the app for
- Chord Drill (says a chord name, listens for correct shape/clean ring) — set the
  Difficulty dropdown to Beginner or Novice for this stage's chord set
- Metronome, ramping tempo per drill
